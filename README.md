# LokalLead: Dit Moderne Online Udtryk

Create a premium Scandinavian website for a company called "LokalLead".

IMPORTANT BUSINESS CONTEXT:

LokalLead is NOT a traditional web agency.

LokalLead helps local service businesses see what a modern website could look like before they commit to buying.

The core offer is:

Business owner books a free review.

Business owner provides basic information about their company.

LokalLead creates a custom website concept.

The concept is presented during a meeting.

The client decides whether they want the website built.

The website should communicate simplicity, trust and business results.

Do NOT create the feeling of a large agency.

Do NOT create the feeling of a complicated process.

Do NOT focus on AI.

Do NOT focus on technology.

The website should focus on:

More customer inquiries

Better first impressions

Modern online presence

Simple process

Low risk for the customer

STYLE

Premium Scandinavian design

White background

Navy blue and green colors matching the LokalLead logo

Large whitespace

Professional but approachable

Clean typography

Mobile-first

Modern but not flashy

Generate ALL content in Danish.
